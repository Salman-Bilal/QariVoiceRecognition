About This Project

How It Works

Uses the ECAPA-TDNN deep learning model to extract a 192-dimensional voice fingerprint from your recitation, then compares it against reference fingerprints of 12 professional Qaris.

Training Data

Trained on recordings from 12 world-famous Qaris across 8 Surahs, producing 2,939 audio clips. Achieved 99.83% test accuracy.

Analysis Metrics

Evaluates your recitation on three dimensions: Timing/Rhythm (DTW alignment), Melody/Pitch (pitch contour), and Breath Phrasing (pause patterns).
